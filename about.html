About

Computer scientist, teacher, and researcher working on AI for education at UC Berkeley.

I spent five years at UC Berkeley — B.A. degrees in Computer Science and Economics, then an M.S. in Computer Science — nearly all of it overlapping with teaching. I joined Data 8 course staff in 2022, moved to CS/Data 100, and worked from uGSI up to Lead TA, Head TA, and finally Lecturer.

Teaching at that scale is where my research comes from. My master's thesis, MilOH, is a retrieval-augmented tool that gives TAs a fast read on an incoming office-hours ticket, so their time goes to diagnosis and explanation instead of rediscovering context. I'm equally interested in whether we can understand what these systems are doing at all — a question that led me to survey the interpretability literature.

Elsewhere: hypothesis testing for competitive networks in the Strang Lab, a study of demographic bias in economics publishing, a $25k win at the European Innovation Academy for a wifi-connected medication dispenser, and the computer vision write-ups on this site.

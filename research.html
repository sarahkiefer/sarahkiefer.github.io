Master's Thesis

MilOH: Supporting Teaching Assistants with Retrieval-Augmented Guidance in Office Hours

Advisors
Joshua Hug (advisor) and Narges Norouzi (editor)
Report
UCB/EECS-2026-190
Filed
May 15, 2026
Read the PDF Berkeley tech report

Abstract

Office hours serve as vital instructional settings in large data science courses. In an environment where students access general-purpose language models for debugging and explanations, office hours' true value emerges from human instruction that diagnoses confusion, tailors explanations, demonstrates expert reasoning, and builds on student comprehension.

MilOH functions as a retrieval-augmented tool assisting TAs during office-hour sessions. It combines student ticket data with course materials, Q&A histories, and student code from JupyterHub. The system provides concise guidance helping staff identify potential issues, relevant topics, and instructional directions. Rather than supplanting the TA, MilOH minimizes time spent on routine debugging and information gathering, enabling focus on diagnostic questioning, targeted instruction, problem-solving modeling, and deeper understanding development.

A month-long pilot in Data 100 demonstrated that TAs found MilOH particularly valuable when orienting to unfamiliar tickets and surfacing relevant course materials. The approach represents a human-centered strategy for educational AI support, combining material retrieval with live code context to maintain office hours' instructional distinctiveness while enhancing support scalability and consistency in large data science courses.

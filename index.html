Master's Thesis

MilOH: Supporting Teaching Assistants with Retrieval-Augmented Guidance in Office Hours

M.S. Computer Science · UC Berkeley EECS · UCB/EECS-2026-190 · May 2026
Advised by Joshua Hug and Narges Norouzi

MilOH is a retrieval-augmented tool that assists TAs during office hours, combining student ticket data with course materials, Q&A histories, and student code from JupyterHub. A month-long pilot in Data 100 found it most valuable for orienting staff to unfamiliar tickets and surfacing relevant course materials.
